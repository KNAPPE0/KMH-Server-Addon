kmh_logo.png is the Colony Nexus mark (the world, ring and moon from the Colony Nexus
website), rendered from the same source as the mod icon (KMH-Patch/About/ModIcon.png).
It is Colony Nexus's own artwork, and the
file keeps the name kmh_logo.png. Replace it with your own server logo by dropping a PNG
named kmh_logo.png into KMH-Data/Icons.

Every other icon here is from Google Noto Emoji
(https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji), licensed under the Apache License 2.0
(full text in LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt beside this file).
