Used by the server media resolver to decode WebP images that the KMH
client cannot decode, and to re-encode them as PNG (static) or animated
GIF (animated) so they can be displayed in game.

The Six Labors Split License grants use under the terms of the Apache

"a For-profit company/individual with less than 1M USD annual gross
revenue". KMH is distributed under that clause.

Version note: the 3.1.x line is pinned deliberately. ImageSharp 4.x
introduces a build-time license-key requirement (the build fails with
"No Six Labors license found" unless SixLaborsLicenseKey,
SixLaborsLicenseFile, or a sixlabors.lic file is supplied). 3.1.x has
no such requirement and provides the animated-WebP decoding and
animated-GIF encoding the resolver needs. Do not upgrade this
dependency to 4.x without first obtaining a license key.
